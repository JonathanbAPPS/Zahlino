2. Principle

The provider of Zahlino does not collect any personal data. The app keeps no user account, requires no registration, and contains neither advertising nor analytics, tracking or any other third-party components.

3. Data in the app

In order to run the app, the following data is stored exclusively on the device: completed lessons, XP, streak, badges, game records and the practice statistics of the past weeks. Storage uses the standard facilities of iOS within the app's own storage area.

This data serves solely to show learning progress inside the app. It is not transmitted to the provider or to third parties. It is contained in a device backup that you create yourself.

Retention period: for as long as the app is installed. Under “Settings → Reset progress” the data can be deleted immediately at any time; uninstalling removes it from the device entirely.

4. Network connection

Practising, playing and all progress work without an internet connection. A connection is established solely for Game Center, and only when an explicit action triggers it.

5. Game Center

Using Game Center is voluntary. Only after signing in does the app transmit scores and achievements to Apple; the player name used is the one you have set with Apple yourself. Apple is the controller for this processing; Apple's privacy policy for Game Center applies (apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/game-center).

The legal basis is consent given by signing in explicitly, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR. The connection can be ended at any time under “Settings → Game Center → Disconnect”; after that the app transmits nothing further.

Zahlino does not store the Game Center player ID and does not pass it on. There is no messaging function between players; you are never matched with strangers, and invitations are limited to friends you choose yourself.

6. This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When the site is accessed, GitHub processes technical connection data, including the IP address, date and time, the page requested and browser and system information. This is necessary for the page to be delivered at all; the legal basis is the legitimate interest in secure operation, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Data may be transferred to the USA in the process. As a safeguard within the meaning of Art. 46 GDPR, GitHub relies for such transfers on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses (Implementing Decision 2021/914). The provider of this site has no access to this processing and does not evaluate it. Details: docs.github.com/privacy

The site sets no cookies, embeds no third-party fonts, images, maps or videos, and uses no analytics tools.

7. Disclosure to third parties

The provider does not disclose any data to third parties.

8. Children

The app is also aimed at children. For that reason it contains no advertising, no in-app purchases and no way of contacting other people.

9. Rights of data subjects

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), to rectification (Art. 16), to erasure (Art. 17), to restriction of processing (Art. 18), to data portability (Art. 20) and the right to object (Art. 21). Consent once given may be withdrawn at any time with effect for the future.

Since the provider itself stores no personal data, access and erasure have nothing to act on as far as the app is concerned — the data is on your device and can be deleted there by you. For the data processed through Game Center, Apple is the right point of contact.

10. Right to lodge a complaint

You have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the Member State of your residence, place of work or the place of the alleged infringement (Art. 77 GDPR). A list of the German supervisory authorities is available at bfdi.bund.de.
