Frequently asked questions

Is a user account required?
No. The app requires neither registration nor a sign-in. Your progress is stored exclusively on the device.
Is an internet connection needed?
No. The curriculum, practice and every game work completely offline. A connection is established solely for Game Center, and only when an explicit action triggers it.
Are there any costs?
No. The app is free and contains no in-app purchases, no subscriptions and no advertising.
Is Game Center mandatory?
No. Everything apart from the leaderboards is available without Game Center. After signing in, invitations are possible only to friends you choose yourself; you are never matched with strangers, and there is no messaging function between players. The connection can be ended at any time under You → Settings → Game Center → Disconnect.
How do I reset my progress?
Under You → Settings → Reset progress. This deletes completed lessons, XP, streak, badges and game records. It cannot be undone. Independently of that, the placement test alone can be taken again under Retake placement.
Can sounds and vibration be turned off?
Yes, separately from each other under You → Settings with the Sounds and Vibration switches. The device's silent switch is always respected, and audio playing in other apps is never interrupted.
Can the appearance be changed?
By default the app follows the device setting. Under You → Settings → Appearance you can instead choose Light or Dark for good.
Which languages does the app speak?
German and English. By default the app follows the language of your iPhone; under You → Settings → Language you can switch to Deutsch or English for good.
Is my progress carried over when I change devices?
Yes, provided a device backup is restored: your progress lives in the app's own storage area and is contained in a backup you create yourself. Transfer through a user account is not possible, as the app keeps no account.
What are the system requirements?
iOS 26 or later. Zahlino is an iPhone app.
What age group is the app for?
The curriculum runs from the small times tables up to upper secondary level, roughly from third grade onwards. A placement test at the start finds the right entry point.
A question is worded wrongly or marked incorrectly.
Reports like this are expressly welcome. A screenshot of the question concerned, together with the answer you expected, is helpful.
